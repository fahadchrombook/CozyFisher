Privacy Policy

Last updated:
Short version: We collect no personal data at all. Your game progress stays on your own device. That's it — read the details below. 🌊

1. Who We Are

Cozy Fisher is a cozy fishing game developed by TroyHouse Games. You can reach us any time at the email address at the bottom of this page. We read every message.

2. We Do NOT Collect Personal Data

Cozy Fisher collects no personal information whatsoever:

  • No names, emails, or accounts
  • No addresses or location data
  • No advertising IDs or device identifiers
  • No analytics, tracking, or profiling of any kind
  • No recordings of your gameplay

We do not know who you are, where you are, or what you do in the game — and we never will.

3. What IS Stored, and Where

The only data in Cozy Fisher is your game save file, which contains:

  • In-game money and statistics (fish caught, casts thrown, records)
  • Progress: islands unlocked, upgrades, collection book, decorations
  • Settings (music and sound volume)

This save lives entirely inside your own browser (local storage, or your platform's cloud-save service if you play on a game portal). It is anonymous game data only — it contains nothing that can identify you, and it never leaves your device through us.

4. How Your Data Is Protected

  • Your save never leaves your device — we run no servers and receive nothing from you.
  • No accounts exist — so there are no accounts to hack and no databases to leak.
  • Nothing is shared or sold — we have no data to give to anyone, ever.
  • You are always in control — you can erase everything instantly using the RESET SAVE button in the game's pause menu, or by clearing your browser data.

5. Third Parties

If you play Cozy Fisher on a hosted platform such as CrazyGames, that platform may process anonymous technical data (such as browser type or approximate region) under their own privacy policy. That data handling is outside our control — we ourselves collect nothing.

6. Children

Cozy Fisher is family-friendly and safe for all ages. Because we collect no data at all, no information about children (or anyone) is ever gathered, stored by us, or shared.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we ever change this policy, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date. Since we don't collect data, any change would be small — but you'll always find the current version here.
